About

I have hands-on experience in machine learning, data handling, genomics and transcriptomics, from NGS pipelines and single-cell RNA-seq to classifiers validated with cross-validation and ROC/AUC.

What drives me is finding real significance in the data I analyse: results that hold up statistically and mean something biologically. I want to apply this approach to translational healthcare research, from biomarker discovery and precision medicine to drug and vaccine development, with a particular interest in cancer and rare diseases, where better analysis can bring answers to patients sooner.

🧬 Bioinformatics & computational biology

  • Transcriptomics: bulk and single-cell RNA-seq
  • Differential expression and method benchmarking
  • Wet-lab background (Biotechnology Engineering): I read data in its experimental context

📊 Data science & machine learning
